# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Small and medium business owners (restaurants, shops, services, professionals) who need a professional website but don't have technical staff. They want to look credible online and get more customers.

## Product Purpose
Pilot Software LLC is a web studio that designs and builds websites for businesses. The site's job: convince a business owner that Pilot Software can make them look professional online, and get them to reach out.

## Brand Personality
Capable, direct, warm. Not a faceless agency. Not a freelancer. A small team that ships fast and cares about the result.

## Anti-references
- Dark-mode SaaS landing pages with gradient text and particles (the current site)
- Generic "web agency" templates with stock photos of people pointing at laptops
- Over-designed portfolio sites that prioritize aesthetics over clarity

## Design Principles
1. The site IS the portfolio piece — if it doesn't look great, nobody believes we can make theirs look great.
2. Clarity over cleverness. A business owner should understand what we do in 3 seconds.
3. Earn trust with specifics, not buzzwords.
4. Every section moves toward the CTA.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA. Good contrast. Works without JS. Reduced motion respected.
