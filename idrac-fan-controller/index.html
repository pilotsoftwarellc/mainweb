iDrac Fan Controller

A native iPhone utility for compatible Dell PowerEdge servers. Control iDRAC fan speed over Wi-Fi or VPN, restore automatic fan control, and monitor available hardware sensors without a cloud account.

Support Privacy Policy
Native fan control

Apply manual fan speed presets and restore automatic iDRAC control directly from your iPhone.

Hardware monitoring

View available CPU temperature, fan, power, memory, storage, and system summaries where supported by your iDRAC.

No cloud backend

The app connects to your own iDRAC on your local network or VPN. No ads, no accounts, no data collection.
