Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 19, 2026

iDrac Fan Controller is designed to run locally on your iPhone and communicate directly with your own iDRAC endpoint. Pilot Software LLC does not collect, track, sell, or share personal data from this app.

Data Collection

The app does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, diagnostics, or usage data for Pilot Software LLC.

Credentials and Local Storage

You may enter an iDRAC IP address, username, and password. These values are used only to connect to the iDRAC endpoint you provide. If you choose to save login information, the app stores it locally on your device using iOS storage mechanisms such as Keychain and UserDefaults. Pilot Software LLC does not receive these credentials.

Network Communication

The app sends IPMI and hardware-monitoring requests directly from your iPhone to your own iDRAC over your local network or VPN. There is no Pilot Software cloud service for this app.

Third Parties

The app does not include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

For support, visit the support page or open a GitHub issue from that page.
