Requirements

  • A compatible Dell PowerEdge server with iDRAC enabled.
  • Your iPhone must reach the iDRAC IP address over the same Wi-Fi network or through VPN.
  • Fan control uses IPMI/RMCP+ on UDP port 623.
  • Hardware sensor details may use Redfish over HTTPS when supported by your iDRAC.

Troubleshooting

  • Confirm the iDRAC IP address, username, and password are correct.
  • Make sure local network or VPN routing allows traffic from the iPhone to the iDRAC.
  • If fan control works but sensors are slow, check whether Redfish is enabled and responsive on the iDRAC.
  • If the app cannot connect, verify UDP 623 is not blocked by your router, firewall, or VPN policy.
