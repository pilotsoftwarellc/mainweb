AI Agents & Automation

AI that works for your business, not just talks to visitors.

There's a big difference between a chatbot and an AI agent. A chatbot answers questions. An AI agent can work on tasks, process information, and work with your systems.

Chatbot vs Custom AI Agent

AI Chatbot

Talks with visitors on your website. Answers common questions, explains your services, and captures contact information. Best for: businesses that want a helpful digital front-desk available around the clock.

Custom AI Agent

Built around your actual business workflows. Can perform multi-step tasks, process information, connect to your tools, and automate repetitive work. Best for: businesses that want AI to do real work, not just talk.

Custom AI Agents

Built around how your business actually works.

  • Customer support workflows
  • Lead qualification & follow-up
  • Internal knowledge assistants
  • Repetitive task automation
  • Form and data processing
  • Multi-step business processes
  • Integration with compatible systems

FAQ

Common questions.

What's included in the $69.99 website?
A professional one-page website with your branding, products or services, contact info, business hours, location (if applicable), phone/message buttons, social media links, basic SEO, and fast loading optimization. Includes a .com domain for the first year and professional email. Hosting is free the first month, then $14.99/month.
What happens after the free hosting month?
After the first month, hosting is $14.99/month for standard websites. The Website + AI Chatbot package is $29.99/month. Hosting includes your server space, SSL certificate, automatic backups, basic monitoring, and professional email. You're never locked in — you can cancel anytime.
What's the difference between an AI chatbot and a custom AI agent?
An AI chatbot talks with visitors on your website — it answers questions, explains your services, and captures contact info. A custom AI agent is built around your actual business workflows. It can perform multi-step tasks, process information, connect to your existing tools, and automate repetitive work. Think of a chatbot as a digital front-desk and an AI agent as someone who actually does the work.
Can you update my existing website?
Yes. Our Website Refresh service ($49.99 starting) is specifically for businesses that already have a website but need a modern look, better mobile experience, updated branding, or improved layout. It's not a full rebuild — it's a targeted refresh to make your existing site work better.
Can you build custom software for my business?
Yes. We build custom web applications, internal business tools, customer portals, dashboards, and admin systems. Instead of forcing your business to adapt to generic software, we build software around how your business actually works. Every project is different — contact us to discuss what you need.
Can AI automate tasks in my business?
Often, yes. We help businesses find repetitive tasks — data entry, follow-ups, form processing, reports — and automate them. We also build AI tools trained on your own data (your procedures, knowledge base, products). The key is finding the right tasks to automate and making sure they actually save you time.
Do you provide hosting?
Yes. We host what we build — websites, apps, and applications. Hosting includes server space, SSL certificates, automatic backups, monitoring, and direct support from the people who run your site. First month is free on our standard plans.
Can you help with servers or business networks?
Yes. Beyond websites, we set up and manage servers (VPS and dedicated), configure networks, set up security, and handle infrastructure for businesses that need more than just a website. This includes Linux servers, application deployment, SSL/TLS, monitoring, and network design.
Do you work with small businesses?
Yes — small businesses are who we build for. We write in plain language, not jargon. We focus on practical solutions that make sense for your budget and your growth stage. If you need technology that actually helps your business, we can help.
