Little Tips Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

Overview

Little Tips is designed for early communication and learning. The app does not include ads, tracking, accounts, or personal data collection.

Data Collection

Little Tips does not collect personal information from users.

  • No ads
  • No third-party tracking
  • No user accounts
  • No external analytics
  • No personal data collection

Local App Data

Little Tips may save local preferences on the device, such as language, voice settings, onboarding status, and app preferences. This information stays on the device and is not sent to Pilot Software.

Purchases

Little Tips Plus purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple may process purchase information according to Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

Children

Little Tips is designed to be child-friendly. It does not collect children's personal information.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email pilotsoftware@icloud.com.
